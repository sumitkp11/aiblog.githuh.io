## Generative AI
- Large language models or LLMS encapsulates the linguistic and semantic relationships between the words and phrases in a vocabulary.
- The model can use these relationships to reason over natural language input and generate meaningful and relevant responses.
- It is often trained on internet-scale data, to generate content in response to user prompts.

## Working of Generative AI
- GenAI models learn by analyzing huge volumes of data known as machine learning.
- The architecture behind Generative AI is called as transformer architecture.

### Advantages of Transformer-based models:
- It process entire documents or conversations, maintaining context for more accurate summaries and insights.
- Transformers handle data in parallel, making them fast and suitable for enterprise workloads.
- They work across domains like text, images, code for flexible foundation for business innovation.

### Use case of Generative AI
- generate personalized email campaigns
- automate responses to common inquiries
- summarize financial reports or generate insights
- draft clinical documentation or patient summaries.
- simulate product designs or optimize supply chains

## Types of Generative AI Models
- Large Language Models - Chatbots, content creation (GPT-4.1, Cluade)
- Code Models: Code completion, debugging (codex-mini)
- Diffusion Models: generate images from text prompts (DALL_E, Stable Diffusion)
- Multimodal models: Interactive assistants (GPT-4o)
- Domain-specific models: Legal, healthcare, finance (Phi-4, DeepSeek)

## Challenges in Generative AI
1. It can produce information that sounds correct but can be factually wrong or entirely fabricated. This challenge can be reversed by investing in fact-checking systems.
2. It may produce different answers to similar prompts, making predictable performance difficult. Inconsistent outputs can disrupt workflows and decision-making.
3. Bias occurs when AI reflect prejudices or imbalances presents in its training data. It introduces ethical and legal risks that can damage brand reputation and erode customer trust.
4. It raises data privacy, energy consumption issues.
