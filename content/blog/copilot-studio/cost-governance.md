## Role of Admins
- Admins watch the same Copilot Credit consumption.
- They fund the capacity
- Govern how the capacity is shared and used.
- Copilot Credits are pooled at tenant level.

## Funding models
- Pay-as-you-go: Usage is billed to an Azure subscription after the billing month. There is no upfront commitment.
- Prepaid capacity: Organization purchase Copilot Credit packs in advance. There is an upfront commitment. Credits are bought before consumed.

## Admins that govern
- Microsoft 365 admin center
- Power Platform admin center
