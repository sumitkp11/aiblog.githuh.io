## Tips and Tricks in Microsoft Copilot Studio

# Tips
1. To change the agent's introduction: Open the Conversation Start topic -> Edit the Message node with your custom message.
2. For Teams or Microsoft 365 Copilot, up to 10 suggested prompts can be configured for end users to choose from to start a conversation with the agent.
